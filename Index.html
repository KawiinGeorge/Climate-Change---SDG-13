The Climate Crisis

2023 was the hottest year ever recorded. Heatwaves are becoming more dangerous and deadly.

What is SDG 13?

SDG 13 calls for urgent climate action. It’s time to reduce emissions, protect the vulnerable, and build a future where nature and people thrive together.
